01 / HR

Core HR that keeps every record in order.

A single, secure home for employee information, so HR spends less time chasing paperwork and more time supporting people.

  • Employee directory with roles, reporting lines and org chart
  • Digital onboarding with checklists, e-signed letters and document upload
  • Role-based access so people see only what they should
  • Reminders for probation, contract renewals and document expiry
02 / Payroll

Accurate payroll, on time, every time.

Salaries are calculated from real attendance, approved leave and reimbursements, with statutory deductions handled for you.

  • Flexible salary structures with allowances, bonuses and arrears
  • Statutory compliance for PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS
  • Payslips on mobile, plus bank transfer files in one click
  • Approval workflow before every payroll run is finalised
03 / Invoicing

Bill clients for the work you actually did.

Turn billable hours, milestones and expenses into professional, tax-ready invoices without retyping a single line.

  • Invoices from timesheets and project milestones
  • GST-ready templates with your branding
  • Payment tracking with automatic reminders for overdue bills
  • Recurring invoices for retainers and subscriptions
04 / Expenses

Expense claims without the shoebox of receipts.

Employees photograph a receipt, and the claim routes to the right approver. Approved amounts are reimbursed with the next payroll.

  • Receipt capture with AI-assisted detail extraction
  • Policy limits by category, grade and project
  • Multi-level approvals on mobile
  • Rebill to clients by tagging expenses to a project
05 / Attendance

Know who's in, without the guesswork.

Accurate time records for office, field and remote teams, ready for payroll at month end.

  • Mobile clock-in with geofencing and selfie check
  • Biometric device sync for existing hardware
  • Shifts and rosters, including overtime and night shifts
  • Regularisation requests for missed punches
06 / Leave approval

Time off, requested and approved in seconds.

Employees see their balance before they ask. Managers see who else is away before they approve.

  • Custom leave policies with accrual, carry-forward and encashment
  • Approval chains with delegation when a manager is away
  • Team calendar and holiday lists by location
  • Automatic sync with payroll and project capacity
07 / Project management

Keep work, people and budgets aligned.

Plan tasks, assign the right people, and watch hours and costs land against each project as they happen.

  • Tasks, milestones and board or Gantt views
  • Timesheets logged against projects and tasks
  • Resource planning that respects approved leave
  • Profitability reports per project and client

How it connects

One action, everywhere it matters.

  1. Clock in

    Attendance records the day from mobile or a biometric device.

  2. Log the work

    Hours land on the right project and task, ready for billing.

  3. Approve

    Leave, expenses and timesheets are approved by managers in one tap.

  4. Pay and bill

    Payroll runs and client invoices are generated from the same data.

Built for mobile

The whole HR office, in every pocket.

Employees clock in, apply for leave, submit expenses and download payslips from their phone. Managers approve requests on the move. It works on Android, iOS and any browser.

  • Employee self-service for profiles, payslips and documents
  • Manager inbox for every pending approval
  • Push notifications for approvals, payroll and reminders
